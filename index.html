FAQ

What is Sand Cohort1?

Sand Cohort1 is how Sand is hiring its first forward deployed engineers in the US. These are full time roles at Sand. Engineers who join work on real client projects across water, energy, telecom and cities.

Why a weekend?

This role needs more than an interview. The weekend lets candidates show how they work with a team on a real problem, and gives them a clear picture of Sand before they decide.
